About raecantwrite

the blogger, the blog, and the ever-growing TBR pile

Hi, I'm Rae! The blog name is a little joke — I'd much rather read than write, but here we are, writing about reading. raecantwrite is my cozy corner of the internet for honest book reviews, reading thoughts, and weekly bookish rambles, brought to you in collaboration with Bronston Canadian Academy.

What you'll find here

Weekly posts: honest reviews rated in book spines (not stars — we're like that here), bookish thoughts and discussions with interactive polls, and the occasional peek at my reading life. Everything I post here also lives in shorter form on my socials, so follow along wherever you like to lurk.

Find me everywhere: I'm on Goodreads, Instagram, and TikTok — come talk books with me. I reply to everything, because talking about books is the whole point. ♡
