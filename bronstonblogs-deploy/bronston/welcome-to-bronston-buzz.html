Welcome to the New Bronston Buzz

same community, fresh coat of (pastel) paint — by rae · july 2026 · 3 min read

Hello and welcome! If you've found your way here from Bronston Canadian Academy's Instagram or Facebook — hi, you're in the right place. This is Bronston Buzz, the new and improved home for posts, projects, and stories connected to our school community. Consider this post the ribbon-cutting: a quick tour of what's new, what's coming, and how you can be part of it.

So, what's new?

Pretty much everything you're looking at! The blog has been rebuilt from the ground up with a whole new look — warm pastel orange, soft navy, hand-drawn illustrations, and little touches everywhere (try tapping things; some of them tap back). More importantly, it's now built to grow: every Bronston-related post will be archived in the Posts tab, so nothing gets lost in a feed, and there's a new post landing regularly.

Meet the Reading Room

Here's my favorite addition: see that 📚 The Reading Room tab at the very top of the page? That's the blog's second wing — a cozy, earthy-pastel corner where I review books, ramble about reading, and rate everything in book spines instead of stars (we're like that here). It launched with a big question — is reading a dying art? — and new bookish posts arrive weekly. If you've ever wanted a book recommendation from someone who annotates too much, that's your door.

Two corners, one blog: Bronston stories on this side, bookish rambles on the other. Wander freely between them.
— the house rules
