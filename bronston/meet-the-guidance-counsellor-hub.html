Meet the Guidance Counsellor Hub

student support · november 2026 · 4 min read

Every Bronston student knows the first kind of question. It sounds like this. How do I solve this problem? Why is my answer different from the example? Those questions belong in your courses, and they have always had a home. But there is a second kind of question, and until now it has had to wander. Am I taking the right courses for the program I want? When do Ontario university applications actually close? Where do scholarships really come from? It sounds less like homework and more like your future, because it is. This week, that second kind of question got a home of its own.

Welcome to the Hub

The Bronston Guidance Counsellor Hub is a new space inside Canvas, run by the Bronston Guidance Counsellor Team, for our online students and our partner school students. Students who have been active in their courses recently were enrolled this week, so if you are one of them, check your Canvas and accept your invitation. Inside, you will find a Start Here welcome and a set of boards, one for each kind of guidance question, with resource pages beside them.

Think of it as the guidance office, rebuilt for a school that lives in many time zones at once. No lining up in a hallway. No wondering which teacher to ask. You post your question on the board that fits it, and the Guidance Team answers there, where the next student with the same question can learn from it too.

What you can ask there

The boards cover the questions students ask us most. There is a board for OUAC and applying to Ontario universities, and another for applying to universities and colleges outside Ontario. There are boards for scholarships and student loans, including what international students should know, and a board for using your OSSD in other countries. There is a board for choosing your courses and your pathway, including which Bronston courses you need for a specific university or college program, and a board with general information about applying for a study permit. There is a board for preparing for college and university, which points students to our free Foundations for Academic Success course, a board for questions about how Bronston programs work, from pacing to report cards and transcripts, a board for tutorial support and the Bronston AI Tutor, and a board for other questions about student life that fit nowhere else.

One small note on scholarships, because it matters. Scholarships come from universities, colleges and outside organisations, so that board is about helping you find them and apply well.

Boards are shared, private stays private

A few honest ground rules, so the Hub works well for everyone. Board questions are visible to all Hub students, so a board is a shared space and not a private one. You may post anonymously if you prefer, but an anonymous post hides your name from everyone, including the counsellor, so nobody can follow up with you privately on it. Anything personal, anything about your own account, and personal immigration matters belong in a private message through the Canvas Inbox instead, where they will be handled one to one.

The other lines are simple too. Course work questions stay in your courses, or come through the Canvas Inbox as they do now. The Bronston AI Tutor remains the place for tutoring help with your learning. The Hub is for guidance.

And the timing. The Guidance Team answers questions Monday to Friday, so a question posted on Friday is answered the following Monday. Asking early is always allowed, and usually wise. Most guidance questions are small in September and very large in the week of a deadline.

No question about your future is too small to ask early.
