November is almost over, and my TBR is not. This is the SHORT list from my Novella November reel, all 40 short books and novellas in one place, so you can save it and come back to it.

Comment SHORT on the Instagram reel and the list will be sent to your DMs. Or just save this post and work through it at your own pace.

These are the books I reach for when I want a full story without a 400 page commitment. Some are true novellas, some are just short novels, and a few sneak a little past 200 pages. I kept them in because they still read fast, and they still feel like a win when you finish one.

Page counts are for common paperback editions and editions vary by a few pages, so your copy might be a little different from mine. Start with the 8 from my video if you want the quickest win, then pick a shelf below and go from there.

From my video, start here (8)

1. The River Has Roots

by Amal El-Mohtar · 144 pages

Two sisters sing to enchanted willows on the edge of Faerie, until a Faerie lover and a jealous suitor put their bond at risk. Fantasy, romantic, a little dark.

2. Binti

by Nnedi Okorafor · 96 pages

Binti is the first of the Himba people accepted to Oomza University in space. Her journey turns dangerous fast. Hugo and Nebula winner for Best Novella.

3. Terminal Boredom

by Izumi Suzuki · 224 pages

Seven punky, pitch black stories of Japanese science fiction, feminist dystopia, gender, isolation and screen obsessed teens.

4. The Vegetarian

by Han Kang · 183 pages in the UK edition

After a nightmare, Yeong-hye stops eating meat, and her quiet refusal unravels her marriage and family. Haunting literary fiction.

5. Fahrenheit 451

by Ray Bradbury · 190 pages in the 50th anniversary edition

Fireman Guy Montag burns books for a living, until he starts questioning everything. Classic dystopia about censorship.

6. Strange Houses

by Uketsu · 208 pages in English

A writer and his architect friend study a floor plan with a dead space and a windowless childs room, and realise the house may have been built for murder.

7. The Sorrows of Young Werther

by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · 176 pages

Told in letters, a young artist falls hopelessly in love with Lotte, who is engaged to another man. A short classic about unrequited love.

8. Our Wives Under the Sea

by Julia Armfield · 228 pages

Leah returns from a deep sea expedition changed, and Miri watches the wife she loves slowly turn toward the sea. Love, grief and body horror.

Fantasy novellas (6)

9. Every Heart a Doorway

by Seanan McGuire · 169 pages

Children who have returned from portal worlds try to fit back into ordinary life, until a murder at their school forces them to look closer.

10. A Psalm for the Wild-Built

by Becky Chambers · 160 pages

A tea monk in a quiet, hopeful future meets a robot who asks what people need. Gentle, cozy and thoughtful.

11. The Emperor's Soul

by Brandon Sanderson · 175 pages

A forger who can rewrite the past of objects is asked to forge a new soul for the emperor, in 100 days, or die trying.

12. The Deep

by Rivers Solomon · 166 pages

The water breathing descendants of pregnant enslaved women thrown overboard build a life under the sea, until one of them is chosen to remember their history.

13. The Ballad of Black Tom

by Victor LaValle · 149 pages

A Black hustler in 1920s New York gets pulled into a dark occult world. A sharp, angry retelling of Lovecraft.

14. The Tea Master and the Detective

by Aliette de Bodard · 96 pages

A scholar and a sentient spaceship solve a murder in a Vietnamese inspired space empire. Short, clever and atmospheric.

Science fiction novellas (5)

15. All Systems Red

by Martha Wells · 156 pages

A self aware security robot who would rather watch TV than protect its humans gets pulled into a mystery on a distant planet. Funny and fast.

16. The Word for World Is Forest

by Ursula K. Le Guin · 189 pages

Colonists logging a forest planet clash with the peaceful people who live there. A short, angry classic about empire.

17. The Time Machine

by H. G. Wells · 118 pages

A Victorian inventor travels far into the future and finds humanity split into two very different peoples. The book that started time travel fiction.

18. Bloodchild

by Octavia E. Butler · about 40 pages as a standalone novella, 144 pages in the collection

On an alien planet, human men carry the young of another species. A short, unsettling classic about power, consent and survival.

19. The Machine Stops

by E. M. Forster · about 70 pages

People live alone underground, served by a Machine, until it starts to fail. Written in 1909, and eerily close to now.

Horror, thriller and mystery (5)

20. Ring Shout

by P. Djèlí Clark · 185 pages

In 1920s Georgia, Black monster hunters fight the Ku Klux Klan, who are literally monsters. Horror, history and rage, all in one short read.

21. The Haunting of Hill House

by Shirley Jackson · 182 pages

Four people stay in a house that may be haunted, or may be haunting them. Quiet, creepy and still the standard for haunted house books.

22. The Turn of the Screw

by Henry James · 124 pages

A governess is convinced the children she cares for are haunted, or that she is losing her mind. You decide.

23. I'm Thinking of Ending Things

by Iain Reid · 210 pages

A woman drives with her boyfriend to meet his parents, and the trip gets stranger and stranger. Short, tense and deeply unsettling.

24. The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde

by Robert Louis Stevenson · 64 pages

A respectable doctor and a violent stranger are more connected than anyone guesses. A one sitting classic.

Literary fiction and classics (6)

25. The Old Man and the Sea

by Ernest Hemingway · 127 pages

An old fisherman sails out alone and hooks a giant marlin. A short, spare story about endurance.

26. The Metamorphosis

by Franz Kafka · about 52 pages

Gregor Samsa wakes up as a giant insect, and his family has to decide what to do with him. Strange, sad and darkly funny.

27. Animal Farm

by George Orwell · 112 pages

Farm animals overthrow their owner, then slowly become just like him. A short, sharp fable about power.

28. The Stranger

by Albert Camus · 123 pages

Meursault is detached, honest and out of step with everyone around him, until one day on a beach changes everything.

29. Of Mice and Men

by John Steinbeck · 107 pages

George and Lennie dream of a farm of their own, while working as ranch hands in California. Short, tender and heartbreaking.

30. Chronicle of a Death Foretold

by Gabriel Garcia Marquez · 120 pages

Everyone in town knows a man is going to be killed, and no one stops it. A short mystery told backwards.

Translated and international (5)

31. Convenience Store Woman

by Sayaka Murata · 163 pages

Keiko has worked in a convenience store for 18 years and is happy there, until everyone around her decides she needs a normal life. Quiet, funny and sharp.

32. The Strange Library

by Haruki Murakami · 96 pages

A boy goes to return books and gets trapped in a strange library. Short, weird and beautifully illustrated in many editions.

33. The Hen Who Dreamed She Could Fly

by Sun-mi Hwang · 144 pages

A hen who longs to hatch an egg of her own leaves the coop and finds a different kind of family. A short Korean fable about freedom.

34. Before the Coffee Gets Cold

by Toshikazu Kawaguchi · 213 pages

A small Tokyo cafe lets you travel back in time, but only until your coffee gets cold. Four linked, gentle stories about regret.

35. The Little Prince

by Antoine de Saint-Exupery · 96 pages

A pilot stranded in the desert meets a small prince from another planet. A short classic about love, loss and seeing clearly.

Dystopia and short classics to reread (5)

36. The Yellow Wallpaper

by Charlotte Perkins Gilman · about 60 pages

A woman is confined to a room for her health, and starts to see a woman trapped in the wallpaper. A short, furious feminist classic.

37. We

by Yevgeny Zamyatin · 226 pages

In a future city of glass, a mathematician starts to dream and fall in love, and the state notices. The dystopia that inspired 1984.

38. A Christmas Carol

by Charles Dickens · 104 pages

Ebenezer Scrooge is visited by three ghosts on Christmas Eve. Short, warm and perfect for December.

39. The Great Gatsby

by F. Scott Fitzgerald · 180 pages

Jay Gatsby throws lavish parties to win back Daisy Buchanan. A short American classic about longing and money.

40. The Pearl

by John Steinbeck · 96 pages

A poor pearl diver finds a huge pearl, and it brings hope, then greed and danger. A short, spare fable about what we want and what it costs.
